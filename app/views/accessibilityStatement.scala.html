Accessibility statement

This statement applies to content published on Transfer Digital Records (TDR) only.

TDR is run by the Digital Archiving department at The National Archives. It is designed to be used by as many people as possible. The text should be clear and simple to understand. You should be able to:

  • change colours, contrast levels and fonts using your browser or device settings
  • zoom in up to 400% without content becoming unusable
  • navigate most of the website using only a keyboard
  • navigate most of the website using speech recognition software
  • listen to most of the website using a screen reader

The website has been designed to comply with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2 AA standard.

How accessible this website is

An independent accessibility audit carried out by RNIB in November 2025 identified a small number of issues affecting form elements and the site header. These issues have since been fixed and rechecked in March 2026.

We are aware of one remaining accessibility improvement. On some pages within the Judgments section, heading levels are not always structured sequentially. This does not fail WCAG 2.2 requirements, but it may make navigation more difficult for some screen reader users.

Reporting accessibility problems with this website

If you find any accessibility issues that are not listed on this page, or need information in a different format, contact us using the details below.

Feedback and contact information

Email: tdr@@nationalarchives.gov.uk

Write to:

Digital Selection and Transfer team c/o Digital Archiving Department The National Archives Bessant Drive Richmond Surrey TW9 4DU

In your message, include:

  • the web address (URL) of the content
  • your name and email address or contact details
  • the format you need - for example, plain text, braille, BSL, large print or audio CD

Your request will be considered, and you will be contacted within 30 days with the outcome of the request.

Enforcement procedure

If you contact us with a complaint and you are not happy with our response, contact the Equality Advisory and Support Service (EASS).

The Equality and Human Rights Commission (EHRC) is responsible for enforcing the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018 (the ‘accessibility regulations’).

Technical information on this website’s accessibility

The National Archives is committed to making its websites accessible, in accordance with the Public Sector Bodies (Websites and Mobile Applications) (No. 2) Accessibility Regulations 2018.

Compliance status

The service is fully compliant with the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) version 2.2 AA standard.

An independent accessibility recheck carried out by RNIB on 19 March 2026 found that the service met WCAG 2.2 level AA. The recheck covered a representative sample of the main records transfer, metadata and judgments journeys.

The audit recorded:

  • 22 Level A success criteria as passed, with no failures
  • 18 Level AA success criteria as passed, with no failures
  • one remaining advisory issue that does not represent a WCAG 2.2 failure

What we're doing to improve accessibility

We are reviewing heading structures across the Judgments section to ensure heading levels follow a consistent hierarchy. Although this issue does not affect WCAG 2.2 AA compliance, addressing it will improve navigation for screen reader users.

Accessibility is considered throughout the design, development and testing of the Transfer Digital Records service. We regularly review the service against accessibility standards and will continue to address any issues identified through user feedback or future accessibility reviews.

Preparation of this accessibility statement

This statement was prepared on 5 October 2026.

The service was originally audited by RNIB on 7 November 2025. It was independently rechecked on 19 March 2026 against WCAG 2.2 level A and level AA success criteria.
