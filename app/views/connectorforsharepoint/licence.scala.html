The TDR Connector for SharePoint App is provided by The National Archives of the United Kingdom to enable organisations to transfer records from a SharePoint file store to The National Archives for the purposes of permanent preservation under the Public Records Act 1958.

1. Use of the App

(a) This TDR Connector for SharePoint App is only intended to be used by bodies in the United Kingdom that are transferring records from a SharePoint file store to The National Archives for the purposes of permanent preservation under the Public Records Act 1958 (the Act).

(b) This includes both bodies subject to the Act, and bodies which, though not subject to the Act, transfer their records to The National Archives under the power of the Keeper of Public Records to accept responsibility for the safe keeping of records other than public records under s2(4)(e) of the Act.

(c) Users of the TDR Connector for SharePoint App are free to download the App and to install it on their chosen SharePoint tenant or specific site.

2. Purpose of the App

(a) This TDR Connector for SharePoint App is provided to enable The National Archives to carry out its public task activity of transferring public records and their associated metadata for the purposes of permanent preservation under the Act, and for the safekeeping of records other than public records.

(b) For the avoidance of doubt, the TDR Connector for SharePoint App is not made available for re-use and is not licensed by these terms of use to be re-used. It should only be used for the specified purpose of transferring selected records from a SharePoint platform to The National Archives for the purpose of permanent preservation under the Act. The App must not be modified or copied by the user (other than to enable it to be installed when first downloaded), and it must not be used for any purpose other than transferring records to The National Archives via TDR.

3. Privacy

(a) The processing of any personal data of users of the TDR Connector for SharePoint App, and the personal data of any individuals that is contained in the records to be transferred to The National Archives for permanent preservation under the Act, will be carried out in accordance with The National Archives’ published Privacy Notice.

4. Duration

(a) The TDR Connector for SharePoint App is intended to operate for a period of ten years from its time of release, and so may be usable until 31 December 2036.

(b) The National Archives will issue enhanced or revised versions of the TDR Connector for SharePoint App from time-to-time during that 10 year period as needed. Users are responsible for ensuring that they use the latest version of the TDR Connector for SharePoint App provided on the Microsoft SharePoint Store when transferring records to The National Archives.

(c) Users must delete the TDR Connector for SharePoint App when they have completed their transfer of records to The National Archives, and can download the latest version of the App again when they next need to make a transfer of records to The National Archives.
